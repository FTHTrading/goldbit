# GoldBit Portal (goldbit.unykorn.ai)

The high-energy, eccentric consumer and institutional sales system for **GoldBit by Unykorn** — a micro-gold allocation and Controllable Electronic Record (CER) issuing platform backed 1:1 by LBMA 99.99% fine gold and settled on XRPL with BitGo rails.

---

## Key Features

1. **Instant Micro-Gold Quoter ($10 to $1,000+)**:
   - 60-second TTL spot quote locking directly against wholesale bullion APIs.
   - Dynamic real-time calculation of allocated fine milligrams (`XAU_MG`) and fees with zero floating-point arithmetic.
2. **Multi-Rail Checkout Engine**:
   - BitGo USDC deposit address mapping with QR code display.
   - Credit card & Apple Pay simulation.
   - Instant simulation trigger demonstrating the backend settlement webhook and consensus minting on the XRPL.
3. **Eccentric Video Reels & Vault Stories**:
   - High-energy interactive video grid showcasing actual depository vaults, Brink's & Loomis infrastructure, and BitGo multi-sig signing.
4. **Live Proof-of-Reserve Invariant Dashboard**:
   - Real-time gauge of Vault Fine Gold (mg) vs. XRPL Circulating Obligations.
   - Public HMAC-salted physical bar manifest table (`BAR-MASKED-4A7F8B9C1234`).
   - ECDSA oracle attestation signature and Chainlink bridge compatibility.
5. **UCC Article 12 CER Certificate**:
   - Holographic 3D Controllable Electronic Record gold deed card with cryptographic seal and downloadable PDF deed.
6. **Physical Stacking & Courier Pick-Up Progress**:
   - Visual progress bar from milligram accumulation to 10g LBMA bar threshold, with insured armored courier delivery dispatch.
